Replace the year in a single copyright line.

Legal formats are:

* ``YYYY``
* ``YYYY,``
* ``YYYY ``
* ``YYYY-YYYY``
* ``YYYY-YYYY,``
* ``YYYY-YYYY ``

The final year in the string is replaced with ``replace_year``.
