\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\begin{DoxyVerb}Parse four ``Accept*`` headers used in server-driven content negotiation.

The four headers are ``Accept``, ``Accept-Charset``, ``Accept-Encoding`` and
``Accept-Language``.
\end{DoxyVerb}
