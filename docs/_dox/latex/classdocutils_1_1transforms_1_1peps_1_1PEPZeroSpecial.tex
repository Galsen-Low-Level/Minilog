\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\begin{DoxyVerb}Perform the special processing needed by PEP 0:

- Mask email addresses.

- Link PEP numbers in the second column of 4-column tables to the PEPs
  themselves.
\end{DoxyVerb}
